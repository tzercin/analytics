## Published AI-startup measure

The report states that Türkiye had 1,222 active AI startups and that nearly 35%
were founded in 2024-2025. It also defines a startup as a product-focused private
technology company with uncertain product/business-model elements and excludes
tailor-made solution providers.

These are publisher-reported aggregates derived from Startups.watch, not counts
reproduced from a public legal registry. The report does not expose the full
company list, AI-classification rules, observation-level history or a supported
public API. Therefore the count may be cited with its source and vintage but must
not be presented as independently reproduced or as an official registry count.

## Update and reuse limits

- The report series appears periodic, but no fixed annual publication schedule or
  versioned data API was verified. Check the official library for a newer edition
  before every use.
- Public download does not by itself establish permission to redistribute the
  entire PDF, charts or underlying Startups.watch data. Link and cite by default;
  resolve reuse terms before committing copied material.
- Use the report as contextual evidence. A reproducible startup-count analysis
  still needs an accessible company-level universe, inclusion rules and historical
  snapshots.
