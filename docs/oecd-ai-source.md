## Statistical scope

The methodology defines an AI startup/firm as a private company that researches
or delivers all or part of an AI system, or products and services that rely
significantly on AI systems. Classification combines provider categorisation and
OECD methods; coverage is not a census. Published measures can include investment
amounts, transactions, industries and locations depending on the chart/release.

The data describe disclosed/collected financing activity. They omit some deals,
do not cover internal AI investment by public companies, and do not report company
revenue. “Number of VC investments” must not be relabelled “number of startups.”

## Acquisition contract

No supported public raw-data API or downloadable company-level Preqin extract was
verified. Treat OECD.AI charts and publications as aggregate secondary sources:

1. record publication title, release date, coverage end date and access date;
2. record the exact chart/table title, unit, geography, industry and footnotes;
3. cite both OECD.AI and the named underlying data provider;
4. snapshot the publication or chart only where applicable terms permit, otherwise
   retain the URL, checksum of downloaded material and a structured transcription;
5. never scrape or reconstruct company-level records behind a visualisation.

## Revisions, licensing and interpretation

- A publication is a dated vintage. Do not splice values from editions unless
  definitions and source vintages are shown to match.
- OECD terms permit many uses of OECD-owned data with attribution, but explicitly
  require users to check third-party rights. Preqin ownership makes raw-data reuse
  a gate rather than an assumed permission.
- Country comparisons can be distorted by headquarters location, undisclosed deal
  values, multi-country investors, currency conversion and classification changes.
- Use this source for a contextual global benchmark, not as the primary
  reproducible Türkiye AI-startup count.
